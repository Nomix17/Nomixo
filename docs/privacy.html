Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Nomixo is a free, open-source desktop app that runs entirely on your computer. You don’t need an account to use it, and it has no server, analytics, or tracking. This page explains what data the app handles and who else can see it.

Open source

You can verify everything on this page by reading the code at github.com/Nomix17/Nomixo.

What stays on your computer

The following is stored locally on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your library, favorites, watch history, and playback progress
  • Your download queue and downloaded files
  • Your settings and theme
  • The TMDB and Wyzie API keys you enter

The developer of Nomixo does not receive, collect, or have access to any of this.

Third-party services

Nomixo connects to other services to work. These are run by other companies, not by Nomixo, and each has its own privacy policy. When Nomixo contacts them, they can see the request and your IP address.

  • TMDB provides movie, show, and anime information. Nomixo sends your searches and the titles you browse, along with your own API key.
  • Torrentio provides stream sources. Nomixo sends the ID of the title you pick to get a list of available streams.
  • Wyzie provides subtitles. Nomixo sends the title you are watching and your own API key. This is optional.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Peer-to-peer streaming

Nomixo streams and downloads using WebTorrent. This is how peer-to-peer file sharing works: other people in the same swarm can see your IP address and which content you are downloading or sharing. Nomixo has no control over this. If this matters to you, consider using a VPN.

MPV

Playback happens in MPV, a separate player that runs on your computer. Nomixo points it at a local stream and does not send playback data anywhere.

Deleting your data

Everything Nomixo stores is on your computer. To remove it, uninstall the app and delete its data folder.

Children

Nomixo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
